Who are we?

At Booked, we believe stories are meant to be shared. Our platform is designed for book lovers who want to connect, discover, and share their passion for reading. Whether you're here to exchange recommendations, share your opinions, or find your next favorite book, you’ve come to the right place.

How we work

Create profile

By creating an account you are instantly welcomed to the community of Booked. Here you can showcase your favorite books, what you're currently reading and share your progress.

Join the Challenge

Get inspired by our weekly reading challenges tailored to your profile and rediscover the joy of stories.

Connect and discuss

Connect with fellow readers and join Book Clubs to share your opinions about specific books. 

Access personalized book recommendations   

Discover new stories! With our monthly Book Box, you will receive two personalized books based on your taste.
